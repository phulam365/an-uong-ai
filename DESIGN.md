# Modern Bistro Menu Design

## Direction

The public homepage menu should feel like a calm, refined bistro menu for browsing food and drinks. It favors editorial spacing, warm surfaces, clear category browsing, polished image presentation, and elegant order controls over fast-delivery urgency.

## Palette

- Paper: warm ivory page background.
- Surface: quiet cream cards, drawers, and modal panels.
- Ink: deep readable text.
- Muted: secondary descriptions and helper copy.
- Olive: navigation, category state, and structural accents.
- Wine: primary action, price, and selected-order accents.
- Brass: small highlights, counts, and hover warmth.
- Border: soft warm dividers and card outlines.

## Layout

- Desktop keeps a sticky left category rail with a restrained restaurant-menu feel.
- Mobile uses calm horizontal category tabs above a stable one or two column grid.
- Product cards use consistent image ratios, soft borders, concise copy, and clear quantity controls.
- Detail views pair generous imagery with readable ingredients, price, and ordering controls.

## Interaction

- Quantity state remains local to the React page.
- Detail opens from the product card or view action.
- Detail closes from backdrop click, an accessible icon button, and Escape.
- Cart interactions use a composed drawer with readable line items, empty state, and total summary.
